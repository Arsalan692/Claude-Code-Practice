Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our expense tracking service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, as well as the expense data you choose to record — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain the Service, authenticate your account, display your expense history and summaries, and communicate with you about your account.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and we take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties. We may share information only where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Cookies

Spendly may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time. If you would like your data removed from our systems, you can request account deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the contact details provided on our website.
